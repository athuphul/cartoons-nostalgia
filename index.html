Configured Video Lists

The simulator reads its playlist from three files in the data/ folder. You can configure them as either JSON or plain text (one URL per line):

🎵
Cartoon Intros data/intros.json / data/intros.txt

Opening themes, catchy title tracks, and intro sequences.

📢
Commercial Ads data/ads.json / data/ads.txt

Nostalgic 90s/2000s vintage Indian and retro advertisements played during commercial breaks.

🎬
Cartoon Episodes data/episodes.json / data/episodes.txt

Full length cartoons and episodes that play between the intros and the commercial breaks.

💡 How the TV Broadcast Simulator Works:

  1. Broadcast Simulation Mode: Follows genuine 90s Sunday morning TV scheduling: plays a Cartoon Intro → then the full Cartoon Episode → then a Vintage Commercial Break → then the next Cartoon Intro.
  2. Interactive Living Room: The TV screen is embedded directly into the retro CRT television. You can also click the remote control lying on the rug to pick it up!
  3. Zero Dependencies: Runs with standard Python (python3 server.py) or any static HTTP server.
